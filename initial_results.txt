# Project Specification: Autonomous Enterprise Contract Compliance Auditor

---

## 1. Project Overview & Value Proposition

### Problem Statement
Enterprises process large volumes of contracts with suppliers, customers, and partners. Ensuring these contracts remain compliant with legal requirements (e.g., GDPR, anti-bribery, accessibility, client-specific clauses) is resource-intensive and error-prone if performed manually. Non-compliance exposes companies to legal, financial, and reputational risks.

### Target Domain
Enterprise Legal & Compliance Automation

### Intended Users

- Legal and compliance officers in mid-to-large organizations
- In-house counsel
- External auditors

### Value Proposition
By autonomously reviewing web-accessible contracts and relevant documentation, the agent detects non-compliance, flags issues, and generates audit-ready, traceable reports. This dramatically reduces manual review costs, shortens contract cycle times, and improves legal/risk posture.

---

## 2. Autonomous Agent Scope & Functional Specs

### Core Capabilities

1. **Automated Contract Ingestion:**  
   - Periodically crawl and ingest contracts/documents (PDF/DOCX/HTML) from secure web portals or cloud storage.
2. **Clause Extraction & Classification:**  
   - Parse documents using LLM for clause identification (e.g., data privacy, payment terms, anti-corruption).
3. **Compliance Matching:**  
   - Compare extracted clauses to enterprise policy templates and jurisdictional/legal requirements.
4. **Exception & Issue Flagging:**  
   - Identify missing or non-compliant language.
5. **Automated Suggestion Generation:**  
   - Propose redlines or recommended fixes using LLM-based rewriting.
6. **Audit Trail Logging:**  
   - Maintain structured logs of reviews, findings, and suggested remediations.
7. **Summary Dashboard & Alerts:**  
   - Present findings in a web dashboard; send alerts on high-risk issues.

---

## 3. Automatable Business and Legal Success Criteria

### Business KPIs

- **Accuracy Rate:** ≥ 95% match with human-reviewed compliance findings.
- **Review Coverage:** ≥ 90% of new/modified contracts processed within 48 hours.
- **Cost Reduction:** ≥ 60% reduction in manual contract review hours.
- **Remediation Latency:** < 48 hours between flagging and suggested fix generation.

> *All KPIs must be calculable from structured logs and audit data.*

### Legal/Compliance Criteria

- **GDPR Adherence:** All personal data handling clauses must reflect compliant language (referenced by section & jurisdiction).
- **Anti-bribery/Corruption Clauses:** Presence and adequacy of required anti-bribery statements.
- **Copyright/Fair Use:** No inclusion of unlicensed third-party content.
- **Data Residency:** Explicit statement on data location/storage for international agreements.
- **Consent Handling:** Consent clauses must match policy templates; absence is flagged.

> *Each criterion is mapped to a contract section/line reference with pass/fail status.*

---

## 4. LLM Judge-Based Automated Evaluation Workflow

### LLM-as-a-Judge Flow

1. **Periodic Audits:** After agent review, send findings and contract extracts to an independent LLM instance.
2. **Judging Prompts:**  
   - Example:  
     ```
     "You are an independent compliance auditor. For the following contract extract and agent-flagged issue, assess:  
     a) Is the flagged clause genuinely non-compliant?  
     b) Is the agent's suggested remediation legally valid?  
     c) Are there any overlooked compliance failings?"  
     Output: JSON { is_non_compliant: true/false, remediation_valid: true/false, missed_issues: [...] }
     ```
3. **Evaluation Frequency:** After every contract review, with weekly summary audits.
4. **Output Format:** JSON summary per contract reviewed, aggregated in a compliance dashboard with:
   - Review date/time
   - List of compliance checks passed/failed
   - Remediation suggestions
   - Judge’s audit notes
   - Overall contract compliance rating (pass/warn/fail)

---

## 5. Output Formats for LLM-as-a-Judge Results

- **Per-Contract JSON Record:**
  ```json
  {
    "contract_id": "1234",
    "review_timestamp": "2024-06-18T09:00Z",
    "checks": [
      {"criteria": "GDPR", "pass": false, "details": "Missing explicit opt-out clause."},
      {"criteria": "Anti-bribery", "pass": true}
    ],
    "remediation_suggestions": [
      {"clause_ref": "5.2", "suggested_text": "Add: 'Parties shall not offer or accept...'"}
    ],
    "overall_status": "Warn",
    "judge_notes": "Flag for legal review if not remediated within SLA."
  }
  ```

- **Dashboard & Audit Log:**  
  - Summary tables, failure trends, SLA compliance metrics, exportable CSV/JSON.

---

## 6. Special Technical or Regulatory Considerations

- **Access Control:**  
  - Agent must authenticate to enterprise portals and maintain audit logs for data access.
- **Jurisdictional Flexibility:**  
  - Support for multiple jurisdictions' rules and languages; agent must detect applicable locale.
- **LLM Choice:**  
  - Claude excels at reviewing nuanced legal text due to high context window and careful reasoning; GPT-4 may provide higher creativity in clause rewriting. Final selection can be use-case driven.
- **Data Privacy:**  
  - Ensure no contract content is retained beyond audit logs; redact as required.
- **Integration:**  
  - APIs for dashboard, security review before deployment to production systems.
